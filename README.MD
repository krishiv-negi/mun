# MUN Management & Allocation System

## Project Description
Model United Nations(MUN) is an academic simulation of the united nations where participants represent different countries and discuss global issues and ,MUN Management & Allocation System is a web based system designed to make MUN delegate management and committee/ portfolio allocation faster, more organised and less dependent on manual work. The system will collect delegate details such as MUN experience, previous committees, awards and preferences and use this information to suggest suitable committees and portfolios. 


## Project Statement / Objective
Currently MUN delegate and committee information is mostly managed manually using spreadsheets, forms and different files. This makes the process time consuming and can result in mistakes such as duplicate allocations or unsuitable portfolios. The Objective of our MUN Management & Allocation System is to efficiently  manage delegate information  and suggest  suitable committees and portfolios based on MUN experience, availability, committee requirements and previous allocations. This will make the allocation process faster, more organised and less dependent on manual work.

## Major features/Modules
### Module 1. Delgate Management 
Collect, store and manage delegate details.
### Module 2. CV information
Store and organise MUn experience, previous committees, awards and other relevant information.
### Module 3. Committee & Portfolio Management 
Manage committees, available portfolios and committee capacity.
### Moddule 4. Delegate Allocation
Match Delegates with suitable committees and portfolios using their experience and availbility.The allocation also considers factors such as committee capacity and previous allocations.
